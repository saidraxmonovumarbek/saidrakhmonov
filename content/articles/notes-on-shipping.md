Finishing a project teaches different things than starting one. Here are five lessons that stuck with me.

## 1. Scope is the real enemy

The first version should feel almost embarrassingly small. If it does one thing well, people can tell you what the second thing should be.

## 2. Boring technology is a feature

Pick tools you already know for the parts that are not the point of the project. Save your curiosity budget for the one thing that makes it special.

## 3. Details are the product

Loading states, empty states, error messages and keyboard focus are not polish to add later. They are what using your app actually feels like.

## 4. Write things down

A short changelog and a list of decisions saved me many times when I returned to the code after a few weeks away.

## 5. Ship, then listen

Real feedback only starts after launch. Everything before that is a guess.
